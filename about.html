About Footsteps

Footsteps is dedicated to building stronger communities through leadership development, strategic planning, volunteer programmes, and systemic change initiatives. We work alongside organisations, communities, and individuals to create sustainable solutions that deliver long-term impact.

Our mission is to empower communities by providing access to resources, leadership opportunities, and support programmes that inspire positive change and growth.
