About Footsteps

Footsteps is a community-focused organisation dedicated to creating positive and sustainable change through leadership development, volunteer programmes, strategic planning and community support initiatives. We work alongside individuals, organisations and communities to develop opportunities that empower people and strengthen local networks.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower communities through education, collaboration, leadership development and access to resources. We strive to create meaningful opportunities that help individuals and organisations grow and contribute to long-term social impact.

Our Vision

We envision a future where communities are connected, resilient and capable of creating positive change. Through collaboration, innovation and strong leadership, we aim to support the growth of thriving communities across South Africa.

Leadership Development

Leadership is one of the core pillars of Footsteps. Our leadership programmes equip individuals with practical skills, confidence and experience needed to become effective change-makers within their communities.

Volunteer Programmes

Volunteers form an essential part of our organisation. Through volunteer engagement programmes, community members are able to contribute to meaningful projects that improve lives and create lasting impact.
