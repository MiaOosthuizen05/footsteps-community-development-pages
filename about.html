About Footsteps

Footsteps is dedicated to empowering communities through leadership development, volunteer engagement, strategic planning and sustainable community initiatives. We believe that every individual can contribute to positive change when given the right support, opportunities and resources.

Our Mission

Our mission is to strengthen communities by developing leadership skills, encouraging volunteer participation and supporting long-term sustainable growth through practical, community-focused programmes.

Our Vision

We envision thriving communities where people work together, share knowledge and create opportunities that lead to meaningful and lasting social impact.

Leadership Development

Leadership development lies at the heart of Footsteps. We provide mentoring, practical experiences and workshops that help individuals become confident and capable leaders within their communities.

Volunteer Programmes

Our volunteer programmes connect individuals with meaningful community projects where they can gain experience, contribute positively and create lasting impact.

Meet Our Team
